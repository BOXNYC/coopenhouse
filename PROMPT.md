# Prompt

Please write a design doc and mock up a few screens for a service that helps real estate agents cover each other's open houses.

Today, real estate agents who can't make one of their own open houses usually post in their brokerage's Slack or group chat and hope a colleague is free to host it. Other agents would happily cover one when the time and place suit them, but there is no easy way for them to find those open houses.

We'd like you to design a service that connects agents who need an open house covered with licensed agents nearby who are available to host it.

## What we are looking for

1. A **design doc** that considers the features and aspects of the platform: who it's for, the core flows for the agent who needs coverage and the agent who covers, and what you would build as an MVP.

2. A **few demo screens** of the flows you think matter most. There are no points gained or lost for something fully functional or bug free, but a good UI and UX do count.

## Considerations

- Consider how agents might want to be compensated for covering someone else's open house, in whatever form makes the most sense to them.

- Put yourself in the shoes of an agent handing their listing to someone they've never met. What would make you trust them, and how does your design handle it?

- Consider edge-cases, where things could go wrong, handling problems, etc.

- There is a lot of surface area here (maps, calendars, license verification, payments), so go deep on what you think matters most rather than trying to cover everything.

## A few ground rules

Please limit yourself to **3 hours**, and use whatever tools you'd like.

We encourage you to **build with AI**, and we will want to discuss the tools you used.

Please send everything back **by end of day Wednesday, November 25, Eastern Time** (let me know if this timeline is an issue at all).
